Play statistics & privacy

Penguin Shop is made by Dao of Play. This notice covers the browser game at mitohacker.github.io/penguin-shop-threejs. Updated 2 October 2026.

We use Google Analytics 4 to learn whether the game loads successfully, how many visitors start playing, whether they pick up and place penguins, how far they progress, and how much time they actively play. Campaign tags help us identify visits from our YouTube profile. Active play time excludes menus, hidden tabs and prolonged inactivity.

Google Analytics uses cookies and browser identifiers and processes information such as browser/device details and approximate location. We have disabled Google Signals and advertising personalization. Our game events do not include your name, account details, save contents or raw error messages.

You can turn off “Share basic play statistics” in the game’s Settings. The choice is saved in this browser. Disabling it stops this game from sending further Analytics events; it does not erase events already collected. Browser or network blockers may also prevent collection, so our statistics are estimates.

Your game progress and settings are saved locally in your browser. Importing or exporting a save uses a file on your device. We do not send those save files to Analytics.
